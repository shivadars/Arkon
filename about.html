Who We Are

Arkon Medical Systems is promoted and led by Mr. Pothen John and Mr. Anto Thomas, both engineering graduates, whose professional backgrounds and experience span more than thirty years in the medical devices industry.

The Senior management has been successful in creating a viable organizational culture that suits the needs of the business and sending a message to all who matter to the business both internal and external.

The organization takes pride in its tremendous growth in a short span of time which has been achieved through the dedicated effort and commitment to customers and suppliers in the core Kerala market. Team Arkon is committed to succeeding higher being a strong ally to its reputed medical equipment partners and a reliable supplier for its customers.

03

Senior Management:
Mr. Pothen John

Mr. Pothen John has a rich experience of 30+ years in the medical devices industry. Before his entrepreneurial venture at Arkon, he worked for several well known companies in India and South Asia.

In his various professional capacities, Pothen has handled sales and service of products in different segments of medical industry including ophthalmology, IVD, critical care, patient monitoring, cardiology and anaesthesia.

His keen business sense and thorough industry knowledge brings value to the Arkon team.

04

Senior Management:
Mr. Anto Thomas

Mr. Anto Thomas has a career spanning 30+ years in the medical industry. An engineering graduate by training, he has been spearheading successful business operations across Kerala in government, private, and corporate healthcare sectors for over 14 years, establishing a reputable presence and trusted partnerships across major hospital networks.

He brings extensive clinical and technical expertise in patient monitoring systems, anaesthesia workstations, medical gas pipelines, critical care setups, and CSSD installations. With an expansive network across the Kerala market, dynamic leadership, and an unwavering commitment to prompt after-sales support, Anto continues to drive strategic value and dependable healthcare infrastructure for the Arkon team.
