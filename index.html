Who We Are

01

Arkon Medical Systems is promoted and led by Mr. Pothen John and Mr. Anto Thomas, both engineering graduates, whose professional backgrounds and experience span more than twenty years in the medical devices industry. Just as an individual is known by his personality, organizational culture is the personality of the organization. The Senior management has been successful in creating a viable organizational culture that suits the needs of the business and sending a message to all who matter to the business both internal and external. The organization takes pride in its tremendous growth in a short span of time which has been achieved through the dedicated effort and commitment to customers and suppliers in the core Kerala market. Team Arkon is committed to succeeding higher being a strong ally to its reputed medical equipment partners and a reliable supplier for its customers.

Mission Statement

02

Customer satisfaction remains our top priority. Studying our products seriously and taking customer feedback on quality and performance as well as informing the parent company of any inconsistencies tops our agenda.

Senior Management: Mr. Pothen John

03

Mr. Pothen John has a rich experience of 20 years in the medical devices industry. Before his entrepreneurial venture at Arkon, he worked for several well known companies in India and South Asia. In his various professional capacities, Pothen has handled sales and service of products in different segments of medical industry including ophthalmology, IVD, critical care, patient monitoring, cardiology and anaesthesia. Having worked in the service industry for the last six years, with a very reputed organisation he understands the value of after sales service and is committed to executing strategic plans to provide value added services to his customers. His keen business sense and thorough industry knowledge brings value to the Arkon team.

Senior Management: Mr. Anto Thomas

04

Mr. Anto Thomas has a career spanning 22 years in the medical industry. He has been doing successful business in Kerala in Government, private and corporate sectors for the last 14 years and he has made his presence felt across Kerala. He has ample experience in patient monitors, Anaesthesia, medical gas pipe lines, critical care, CSSD, to name a few. Anto’s extensive network in the Kerala market and dynamic personality adds value to the Arkon team.
