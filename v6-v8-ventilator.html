COMEN V6 & V8 Ventilators

ICU Mechanical Ventilator with Double Driven System

Advanced life support platform featuring invasive, non-invasive, and APRV modes. Engineered for stable therapy, fast response, and superior critical care outcomes.

Double Driven System Security

The V6 and V8 ventilators incorporate a dual pneumatic and electronic drive system. Equipped with a high-performance backup turbine, the system guarantees uninterrupted operation and stable ventilation even during central gas supply failures.

This critical redundancy ensures patient safety at all times, making it a reliable cornerstone in high-acuity ICU environments.

Ergonomic & Intuitive Design

18.5" High-Definition Detachable Screen

The V8 features an expansive 18.5-inch high-definition touch display that is fully detachable. It supports extensive physical adjustments—horizontal rotation up to 270° and vertical tilting up to 45°.

This allows clinicians to position the interface optimally in crowded ICU setups, ensuring crucial patient data and waveforms are always clearly visible.

  • • 270° Horizontal Rotation
  • • 45° Vertical Adjustment

Dedicated Neonatal Support

The V6 and V8 are built to handle patients of all ages, from adults down to delicate neonates. They feature specialized neonatal invasive and non-invasive ventilation modes, including Comen's proprietary NIPPV and SNIPPV.

High-precision sensors ensure extremely accurate volume delivery and rapid trigger response, providing gentle yet effective respiratory support for the smallest patients.
