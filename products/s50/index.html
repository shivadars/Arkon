S50

Defibrillator Monitor

An integrated defibrillator monitor with AED and pacing, featuring an IP55 waterproof design, real-time CPR feedback, and versatile power options for all rescue environments.

  • 4-in-1 integration: Manual defibrillation, AED, pacing, and monitoring (ECG, SpO₂, NIBP, CO₂)
  • Multiple power options including a 4700mAh large-capacity lithium battery
  • User-friendly, rounded, easy-to-clean body with a clear interface
  • IP55 dust and water proof rating for demanding environments
  • Real-time CPR feedback and diverse interconnection methods (HDMI, Network port)

Overview

The S50 Defibrillator Monitor provides a full range of functions to meet various life support needs. It integrates manual defibrillation, AED, pacing, and comprehensive monitoring to make life-saving more efficient in both pre-hospital and in-hospital scenarios.

Technical Specifications

FunctionsManual Defib, AED, Pacing, Monitoring
Monitoring ParametersECG, SpO₂, NIBP, CO₂
Power SupplyAC, 4700mAh battery, fixed base, inverter
DurabilityIP55 dust and water proof
ConnectivityHDMI, Network port

Key Features

Discover the advanced capabilities and intelligent design that set our solutions apart.

Comprehensive 4-in-1 Life Support

Integrates manual defibrillation, AED, pacing, and monitoring (ECG, SpO₂, NIBP, CO₂) into a single device, suitable for both pre-hospital emergency care and in-hospital use.

Versatile Power Solutions

Supports independent AC power, a 4700mAh large-capacity lithium battery, fixed base power, and inverter power supply, ensuring continuous operation.

Ergonomic & Resilient Design

Features a clear interface, a rounded and easy-to-clean body, and a thoughtful handle design. Its IP55 dust and waterproof rating ensures reliability across all emergency scenarios.

High-Quality CPR Guidance

Equipped with a CPR sensor that provides real-time feedback on compression rate, depth, and recoil, supporting AHA recommendations for standardized resuscitation.

Extensive Interconnectivity

Supports multiple interfaces including HDMI for high-definition synchronous display expansion and a network port for versatile information interconnection.
